Simple printable trackers for living with ME/CFS, long COVID and other chronic illnesses. They're made by a fellow member of the community, in the same dark Nuchronic style, with light pages inside so they print cheaply at home.

Each one is an instant PDF download in A4 and US Letter. Buy on Gumroad or Etsy, whichever you prefer: it's the same file.

## Good to know

- **Not medical advice.** These are records to support your care. Talk to your GP or specialist service about your symptoms, pacing and treatment.
- **Print as often as you like** for your own use. Please don't share or resell the files.
- **Buying one helps keep Nuchronic free** for everyone.
